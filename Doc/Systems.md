# Systems

## Question Generator

Input:
- table (2–9)
- difficulty

Output:
- question
- 3 answers
- correct answer

Rules:
- Avoid repetition
- Use wrong answers close to correct

---

## Adaptive System

Track:
- errors per multiplication

If high error:
- increase frequency

---

## Combat System

Correct:
- damage enemy

Fast answer:
- critical hit

Wrong:
- take damage

---

## Evolution System

Trigger:
- 10 correct in a row
OR
- mastery > 80%

Effect:
- stat boost
- visual evolution
